The one fast failure is `test_single_phase_remap.py::TestBoxDecayWithEOS::test_remap_matches_fixed_connectivity`,
the cloud session's environment baseline (a), with the identical two
values (`0.4399043770574842 != 0.43990437705748414`). It is NOT caused by
the cloud work: it fails in the same way, deterministically (six runs with
`PYTHONHASHSEED` 0 / 1 / 12345 and `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`), on the exact
hand-off tree (`git archive b5bd379` of ddgclib with `git archive 48d163a`
of hyperct on `PYTHONPATH`), and neither numpy (installed 2026-02-11) nor
glibc (2026-08-21) changed since the hand-off of 2026-10-06 that reported
it passing. I could not reproduce that pass.

Cause, measured with a 40-line probe (scratch, reproduced below): the
callback of the test's `_run` is first called after step 1 (`t = dt`, not
`t = 0`); at that call the vertex set, the iteration order, the dual
volumes and the edge set (208 directed edges) are identical between the
`REMAP` and the `DUAL_ONLY` arm, but the REMAP arm's masses are the
DUAL_ONLY arm's times a factor in `[1.0, 1 + 2.2e-16]` (29 of 41
vertices one ulp off, total mass 1000.0000000000006 against
1000.0000000000005). The step-1 Delaunay rebuild reconnects nothing, and
the conservative remap still reconstructs every mass as
`scale * rho(p_snapshot) * vol` instead of leaving it untouched: the
remap is not an exact identity on an unchanged connectivity. Fix at the
cause, for the workstation (lane V-review may take it, or a lane of its
own): in `_retopologize` with `retopo_remap='conservative'`, when the
rebuild did not change the edge set, skip the mass reconstruction (the
identity is then exact); this moves every remap run by ulps at its
non-reconnecting rebuilds, so the pins that carry `remap='conservative'`
must be A/B-ed and re-pinned with evidence, or the identity shown to be
bit-exact on them. The alternative (comparing the two arms at the true
`t = 0` by evaluating the callback before the first step) keeps the
assertion's intent without a tolerance, but it does not fix the remap.
Do not loosen the equality.

    # probe (run from the ddgclib root)
    from ddgclib.tests import test_single_phase_remap as T
    import numpy as np, warnings
    def run(methods):
        HC, bV, bc_set, dx_min = T._box(); eos = T._eos()
        with warnings.catch_warnings():
            warnings.simplefilter('ignore'); dudt_fn = methods.dudt_fn(HC, mu=T.MU, pressure_model=eos)
        rec = {}
        def cb(step, t, HC_cb, bV_cb=None, diagnostics=None):
            rec.setdefault('m', [float(v.m) for v in HC_cb.V]); rec.setdefault('e', sum(len(v.nn) for v in HC_cb.V))
        with np.errstate(all='ignore'):
            methods.integrate(HC, bV, dudt_fn, dt=0.25 * dx_min / T.C_S, n_steps=1, bc_set=bc_set, callback=cb, pressure_model=eos)
        return rec
