Advanced Control of Martian Electrolysis

Date: October 6, 2026
Prepared by: Varun Maneeram

Executive Summary: This report presents the performance of a Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) reinforcement learning agent controlling an alkaline electrolysis reactor under Martian conditions (3.72 m/s² gravity). By manipulating the cell voltage (E_cell) to exploit the Lippmann electrocapillarity effect, the agent dynamically reduces bubble detachment volume, significantly reducing thermal violations and improving Faradaic efficiency compared to a standard PID baseline.
